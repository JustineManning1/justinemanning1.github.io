How this data becomes decisions

Dashboards don’t save money — the decisions they enable do. Every project below follows the same value chain, and each page shows all four steps explicitly.

1 · Decompose the metric

Blended averages hide the problem. Segment by category, plan, risk tier, or service line until the driver is visible.

2 · Find the lever

Correlate against operational data — tickets, utilization, reason codes — to find the one variable leadership can actually pull.

3 · Price the intervention

Model the revenue saved or cost avoided so the fix competes for resources with a dollar figure, not a hunch.

4 · Prove it moved

Annotate the intervention date on the trend and track the before/after — the dashboard becomes the accountability record.
